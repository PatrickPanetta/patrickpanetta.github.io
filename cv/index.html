CV

Patrick Fabian Panetta lives and works in Berlin. Working across media and post-conceptual strategies, his work has been widely exhibited in galleries, museums, Kunstvereine and biennials. Recent interviews and reviews include Mousse Magazine, Spike Art Magazine, Artforum and FAZ Quarterly.
